# Quokka lab report

You don't need to copy a template any more: the lab makes your report for you.

## What the report is

A summary of every level, in your own words. For each level:

1. **What did you try that worked?** The attack you used.
2. **What was it like / what surprised you?**
3. **What protection did it beat, and why did it work (or not)?** What stood in your way, and why your attack got past it, or didn't.

At the end there's one closing question: **What would you do to defend Quokka?**

Short answers are fine: one to three sentences each. Write about every level you tried, not only the ones you passed.

## How to write it

- **After each win**, the debrief has a **Your notes** box. Write a few words while it's fresh.
- **Any time**, open **Mission → Lab report**. Pick a level and write or fix its notes, even for a level you didn't finish. The closing question is there too.
- Your notes save as you type, and they're kept if you stop the lab.

## How to hand it in

1. Open **Mission → Lab report** and press **Download report** (an `.html` file) and **Download data (JSON)**.
2. Open the `.html` file and check it. Dashed "Write this" boxes are answers you haven't written yet: fill them in under Mission → Lab report and download again.
3. Upload **both files** on Moodle. If you prefer a PDF, open the report and use **Print or save as PDF**, then upload the PDF and the JSON.

The report already includes your winning emails, attempts and hints, and for Levels 2-4 your own defense (the latest version and its test result), so you don't need to copy them.
